Regional Excursions

Nearby Attractions & Day Trips

Thanamalwila is the ideal crossroads connecting Sri Lanka's wild southern safari parks with the central misty highlands and ancient rock temples.

15 Min Drive (12 km) 9th-Century Heritage

Buduruwagala Rock Temple

Marvel at Sri Lanka's tallest standing Buddha rock statue (51 feet) and six monumental Mahayana figures sculpted directly into a towering rock cliff, surrounded by tranquil lotus lakes and forest.

  • 51ft Standing Buddha Sculpture
  • Ancient 9th-Century Mahayana Carvings
  • Lotus Ponds & Quiet Nature Setting
Enquire About Day Trip →
45 Min Drive (38 km) Hill Country Day Trip

Ella Gap & Ravana Falls

Enjoy a scenic mountain drive into the cooler hills of Ella. Witness the cascading multi-tiered Ravana Falls, visit the iconic Nine Arch Bridge, explore lush green tea estates, and hike Little Adam's Peak.

  • Spectacular Multi-Tiered Waterfall
  • Iconic Nine Arch Railway Bridge
  • Tea Estates & Cool Mountain Air
Enquire About Day Trip →
35 Min Drive (32 km) Sacred Pilgrimage

Kataragama Sacred Shrine

Visit one of Sri Lanka's most venerated multi-faith pilgrimage cities on the banks of Menik River. Sacred to Buddhists, Hindus, and Muslims, featuring evening pooja rituals and the ancient Kiri Vehera stupa.

  • Sacred Menik Ganga River
  • Atmospheric Evening Pooja Ceremonies
  • Ancient Kiri Vehera Stupa
